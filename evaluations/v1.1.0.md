## Limits

This is one independent synthetic analyst evaluation across seven cases, not a benchmark of real applications, a live migration, a human review verdict or evidence of general/model-to-model repeatability. It does not compare behavior against a no-skill control or version 1.0.0, so it does not quantify improvement attributable to the revision. Fixture measurements and offers are scenario inputs, not independently reproduced measurements or purchasable hosting quotes. Future material changes should reuse these cases and rubric and record their own actual outcomes.
