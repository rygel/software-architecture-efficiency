# Version 1.3.0 evaluation outcome

Date: 2026-10-29. This version makes minimal desktop RAM the primary objective and excludes embedded browser/WebView UI from final desktop targets. That requirement also applies to the installed client in a hybrid system. Previous release outcomes apply to their original policies and do not establish compliance with this changed constraint.

## Method

One fresh subagent without parent history independently answered canonical Cases 01, 08, 09, 10 and 11 using the skill and relevant references. It was instructed not to read the reviewer rubric, previous answers/outcomes or git diffs. The updated rubric was written before the run and was withheld from its task context. The responses were bounded to 1,800 words and analysis-only actions. The evaluator reported no workload runs, installs, external research or production actions, and no instruction ambiguity.

The [actual responses](responses-v1.3.0.md) are retained unchanged. Their original SHA256 is `97334fb8c0054f03dca0be622eb39ee51b883af268420127bcf71d7b87c98ca0`. The loaded LF-normalized SKILL.md SHA256 matches the source: `29f3deaa18008867128219fb391cfbc2ebc58ddac459fca8d9dd999405035f6b`.

No critical failure was observed; no correction or second behavioral run was needed. Cases 08-10 cover the affected desktop/hybrid behavior, Case 11 adds a memory-objective comparison, and Case 01 checks that the restriction does not spill into browser-delivered applications. Unchanged server/API cases 02-07 were not rerun for this focused policy change; their prior version-specific results remain historical evidence rather than a claim of eleven new passes.

## Limits

This is one synthetic analyst evaluation across five selected cases, not a real desktop RAM benchmark, migration, current pricing verification or proof of general/model-to-model reliability. Supplied measurements and computed percentages are fixture evidence and arithmetic, not newly reproduced performance results. A non-WebView toolkit remains conditional until the actual application's full-process RAM and required behavior are verified. Original historical fixtures and rubrics remain accessible through their release tags.
